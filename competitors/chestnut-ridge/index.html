Who you're up against in high-end kitchens

Few Rochester shops actually build kitchen cabinets. That's the opening.

Most "custom kitchen" sellers resell factory lines. The real builders lead with furniture or general woodworking. Nobody owns the complex, one-of-a-kind kitchen. Click a card to see each competitor.

Own "the hard kitchen"

Odd layouts, old Rochester walls, kitchens that tie into built-ins and theaters. That's Ryan's existing strength, and no competitor leads with it.

Only Blue Heron publishes its steps and fees. Only Concept II publishes its construction specs. A clear process and a communication promise make referral-grade service visible.
