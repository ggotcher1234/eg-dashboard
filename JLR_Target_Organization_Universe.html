Existing Owner Clients Reviewed
What is emerging: JLR appears especially well matched to small and midsize independent utilities, community services districts, and smaller municipalities that own meaningful water/wastewater infrastructure but do not have the internal engineering depth of a large metropolitan system.
Existing-client growth matters: the strongest opportunity may not always be a new logo. Several current JLR clients have visible capital programs where broader services, additional projects, or a larger share of engineering spend may be available.

How to read the priorities

Priority A: strong fit; active business-development attentionPriority B: good fit; cultivate and monitorPriority C: remain in universe; lower immediate priority

Priority is a practical research classification, not a prediction of whether an organization will purchase JLR services. Signals of change, contacts, and procurement access will be evaluated in Questions Three through Five.
