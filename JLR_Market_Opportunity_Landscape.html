Executive overview

What the expanded Question 1 research means for JLR.

The 200-mile Sacramento market can support JLR's growth goal, but success will come from focus and timing. Aging infrastructure provides the dependable base. Chromium-6 and PFAS provide the strongest emerging demand. Planning-stage projects and existing-client expansion offer the fastest route to accessible revenue.

Scope within the engagement: Question 1 establishes where demand exists, why investment is occurring and which market segments fit JLR. Questions 2–5 separately test account fit, live signals, decision-makers and procurement accessibility before a final pursuit priority is assigned.
Best entry point

Planning, feasibility and study-stage work where the engineering path is not yet closed.

Strongest technical fit

Pipelines, pumps, storage, collection systems, condition assessment, planning, CM and inspection.

Important caution

Funding proves demand. It does not prove that an engineering contract is still open.
