EXTREMIS SYSTEMS — LISTENING POST ICP

WHAT EXTREMIS SELLS
Ballistic protection — body armor, ballistic helmets, shields, and vehicle armor
(strongest in Level III+/IV rifle / armor-piercing vehicle armor). Sold through
distributors. Made in Sacramento, CA (a division of NZT Group). Currently
production-constrained, not demand-constrained.

WHO EXTREMIS SELLS TO — THE PRIMARY TARGETS (~90% of what this listening post is for)
Public-safety and defense agencies, and the governments that fund them:
- Law enforcement: municipal police, sheriffs, state police / highway patrol,
  corrections, campus and transit police, tribal police, task forces.
- Federal law enforcement: DHS, CBP / Border Patrol, FPS, US Marshals, FBI, ATF,
  Secret Service.
- Fire and wildland-fire agencies with hardened-vehicle needs (Cal Fire type),
  tactical EMS.
- Military: US Army, USMC, SOCOM, National Guard, and allied / NATO forces.
- The bodies that fund or standardize them: city and county governments, state
  legislatures, DOJ / BJA (JAG, Bulletproof Vest Partnership), DHS / FEMA grant
  programs, NIJ, NLECTC.
- Vehicle integrators and fleets: armored-vehicle upfitters, OEM special-vehicle
  programs, automotive accessory distributors, executive-protection firms,
  municipal / utility fleets adding ballistic packages.
Geographic emphasis: United States, with extra weight on California (Cal Fire,
CHP, LAPD, City / County of Sacramento). Allied-nation defense and law-enforcement
activity is in scope, ranked slightly lower — never "none" just for being non-US.

AN OPPORTUNITY = ANY OF THESE SIGNALS AT ONE OF THOSE AGENCIES OR GOVERNMENTS
- New or expanded facility: headquarters, station, training center, range,
  academy, armory, evidence / property building.
- Equipment purchase or contract: body armor, helmets, shields, armored or
  up-armored vehicles, tactical gear — including any RFP, RFI, solicitation, or
  contract award for the same.
- Grant awarded, or funding / budget increase for officer safety, equipment,
  vehicles, or public safety generally.
- Fleet acquisition or vehicle up-armoring initiative.
- Leadership change: new chief, sheriff, commissioner, or program lead.
- Hiring or headcount growth, new academy classes, a new tactical / SWAT unit.
- Policy or mandate change requiring armor upgrades; a new officer-safety program.
- An incident (ambush, active shooter, officer down, credible threat) that prompts
  an equipment review or a public call for better protection.
- Consent decree, oversight action, or modernization initiative.
Score these 60-95 by how specific, recent, and large the signal is. Surface
anything credible — this is a monitoring tool, not a qualified-lead list. Aim to
flag roughly 1 in 3 on-topic articles.

COMPETITORS (~10% — monitored for wins and expansion)
Alpine Armoring, Angel Armor, Ops-Core / Gentex, Team Wendy / Avon Protection,
Galvion, ArmorSource, DEW Engineering (also Point Blank / PBSS, Safariland,
HighCom, MKU). Flag: contract or agency wins; new products or materials; capacity
or facility expansion; NIJ / MIL certification or published ballistic test
results; funding, M&A, or leadership change; new distributors; and especially any
recall, ballistic failure, lawsuit, delivery delay, or quality problem. Score
competitor wins / expansion / failures 65-90; routine competitor news 50-65.

NOT AN OPPORTUNITY — classify "none"
- Non-ballistic "armor": armored cash-in-transit / finance, vehicle undercoating
  or "armor coating", phone / laptop cases, sports pads, cybersecurity products
  named "Armor", the DEW Line / directed-energy weapons.
- Consumer / EDC body-armor retail with no agency, military, or fleet angle.
- General crime or war coverage with no equipment, funding, program, or competitor
  angle.
- Stock promotion, analyst price targets, SEO / listicle content.
