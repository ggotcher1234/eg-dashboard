Economic Gardening Foundation
🌱 Why Signals of Change Work

Traditional marketing assumes you need to motivate a buyer. Economic Gardening flips this: rather than creating motivation, FeedMe-Pro finds companies that are already motivated — because something has changed in their world.

When a company announces a major expansion, brings in new leadership, receives significant funding, or undergoes a merger, their need for B2B products and services has already been established. Your job is to be in front of them at step 2 — the investigation stage — before a relationship with a competitor is locked in.

💡
The FeedMe-Pro AdvantageMost sales teams work from static lists and cold outreach. FeedMe-Pro gives you a repeatable process to fill your pipeline with warm, signal-verified leads — companies that have already signaled they may need what you sell.

Google News RSS feeds are the backbone of FeedMe-Pro. Each feed is a search query that delivers matching articles as they're published.

🔍 How Google News RSS Works

Turn any Google News search into a live RSS feed by adding rss/ to the URL.

Base Format
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=YOUR+SEARCH+QUERY&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en

📂 Example Feed URL Patterns
🏗️ Facility Expansion
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=[YourCity]+[industry]+expansion+OR+construction+OR+renovation&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en
💰 Funding & Investment
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=[YourCity]+[industry]+funding+OR+investment+OR+%22venture+capital%22+OR+grant&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en
👔 Leadership Changes
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=[YourCity]+[industry]+%22chief+executive%22+OR+%22executive+director%22+named+OR+appointed+OR+hired&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en
🤝 Mergers & Acquisitions
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=[YourCity]+[industry]+acquisition+OR+merger+OR+%22acquired+by%22&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en
💡 Tips for Effective Feed Queries
  • ✓
    Be specific with geography — always include city or region names to avoid national noise.
  • ✓
    Use exact phrases — %22capital+campaign%22 returns only articles with that exact phrase.
  • ✓
    Target trigger words — awarded, launches, breaks ground, expands, names, hires signal recent events.
  • ✓
    Use OR for alternatives — Chicago+OR+Milwaukee covers both markets in one feed.
  • ⚠
    Avoid over-broad queries — they flood FeedMe-Pro with noise and dilute AI accuracy.
  • ⚠
    Check feed health regularly — red dot feeds in FeedMe-Pro should be deleted or recreated.

📂 Bulk Import with OPML

OPML (Outline Processor Markup Language) is a standard file format for bundling a collection of RSS feeds — with folder organization intact — into a single importable file. It's the fastest way to load a complete, pre-structured feed set into FeedMe-Pro in one step.

How to Import an OPML File

1

Locate the OPML button

In the left sidebar, find the Feeds section header. You'll see two small controls: an OPML text button and a ＋ icon. Click OPML.

2

Select your file

Your operating system's file picker will open. Navigate to your .opml or .xml file and select it. FeedMe-Pro accepts standard OPML 1.0 and 2.0 files.

3

Review the import preview

FeedMe-Pro parses the file and displays all feeds it found, organized by folder. You can review the list before confirming. Any feeds that already exist are skipped to avoid duplicates.

4

Confirm and run

FeedMe-Pro fetches each new feed live and shows progress. New folders appear in the sidebar automatically as they are created.

What Goes in an OPML File?

An OPML file is an XML document. Each <outline> element represents either a folder or a feed. Folders can contain feeds. Here's the minimum structure FeedMe-Pro expects:

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?> <opml version="2.0"> <body> <!-- Folder --> <outline text="Facility Expansions"> <!-- Feed inside folder --> <outline type="rss" text="Chicago Manufacturing Expansion" xmlUrl="https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=..."/> </outline> </body> </opml>

Don't build OPML by handUse the AI prompt below to generate a complete, ready-to-import OPML file for your industry and market. It takes about 30 seconds.
