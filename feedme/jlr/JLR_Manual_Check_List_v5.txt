JLR ENVIRONMENTAL — MANUAL CHECK LIST (v5)

These 23 sources were removed from the automated OPML because they only
worked by restricting Bing search to one specific government or procurement
site, and Bing's RSS search mechanism turned out to be broken (it returns
unrelated filler content instead of real results — confirmed by direct
testing, see the v5 changelog in the OPML itself). There is no working free
RSS/search-wrapper way to auto-watch these specific pages, so they're listed
here to check by hand periodically instead of being silently lost.
