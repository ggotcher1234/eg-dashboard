Temperament Sorter

The Temperament Sorter is a self-assessment based on David Keirsey's temperament theory, which grew out of the same four preference pairs used in the Myers-Briggs model: where you get your energy (Extraversion or Introversion), how you take in information (Sensing or Intuition), how you make decisions (Thinking or Feeling), and how you approach the outside world (Judging or Perceiving). Keirsey grouped the sixteen resulting combinations into four temperaments, each with a distinct pattern of values, talents and communication style. Knowing yours helps you understand how you work best, and why others may see the same situation very differently.
