How and When Can We Compete?

A practical guide for separating funded demand from work JLR can actually pursue—and for getting positioned before the formal solicitation appears.

Executive answer

JLR can compete most effectively when it identifies the purchasing path before a project reaches formal solicitation. The strongest routes are existing-client expansion, upcoming on-call or master-service selections, and project-specific qualifications requests tied to funded capital work.

Important: A funded project is not automatically accessible. JLR must confirm whether design is still unassigned, already held by an incumbent, or limited to a prequalified consultant pool.

The four procurement paths

On-call or MSA

One or more firms are selected for service categories and receive task orders during the contract term.

JLR action

Enter the pool before projects are assigned. Track start dates, option years and board approvals.

Prequalified list

Only firms on an approved roster can compete for later assignments or abbreviated selections.

JLR action

Confirm renewal timing and required disciplines. Submit qualifications before the list closes.

Project RFQ or RFP

The agency advertises a defined project and evaluates the proposed team, experience and approach.

JLR action

Shape the pursuit early, identify partners and prepare relevant project examples before release.

Existing consultant

An incumbent already holds design, program management or an on-call contract that may absorb the work.

JLR action

Find uncovered scopes, teaming roles, contract limits, option expirations or the next re-solicitation.

Pursuit timing over the next 18–24 months

1 · NowCIP adoption, funding, rate action, master planning, compliance deadlines and board direction.
2 · Before solicitationDraft budgets, board discussion, amendments, expiring agreements and prequalification notices.
3 · At solicitationRFQ, RFP, SOQ, interview shortlist or task-order competition. Make the go/no-go decision.
4 · After awardTrack board approval and contract term. Request a debrief and record the next renewal date.

Manual Procurement Watch

FeedMe monitors accessible public signals daily. The sources below require vendor alerts, registration or standing manual review because automated access is restricted. JLR should assign one owner to complete the checks and route relevant notices into the pursuit process.

Recommended routine: Check existing-client bid pages and vendor portals weekly. Check funding and regulatory sources twice each month. Activate email alerts on every portal that offers them.
